Without an index buffer, storing six vertices requires 6 × 2 × 4 = 48 bytes. With an index buffer, we store four unique vertices and six indices, requiring 4 × 2 × 4 + 6 × 4 = 56 bytes.
Therefore, the indexed approach uses 8 more bytes in this case.
